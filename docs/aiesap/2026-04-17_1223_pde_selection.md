## Selection Methodology

Based on the 8-classifier analysis, 7 AIESAPs (an odd number, enabling decisive majority voting) have been selected as Problem Domain Experts. Each AIESAP addresses one or more classifier dimensions rated High or Critical.

The selection ensures:
- Every **Critical** classifier (LS, OM, GM) has at least 2 covering PDEs
- Every **High** classifier (AP, DM, ER) has at least 1 covering PDE
- Cross-cutting concerns are addressed by agents with overlapping remits
- An odd count (7) enables clean majority decisions on contested recommendations
